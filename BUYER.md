# Mascii — what you bought

Turns any video into real-time ASCII art on iOS, rendered on the GPU with
Metal. Zero pixels — a text-mode player, plus the full SwiftUI app around it.

## Run it

Open the Xcode project in this repo, pick your signing team, run on a device
(simulator has no Metal performance headroom for the shader).
